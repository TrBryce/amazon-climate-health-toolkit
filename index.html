REPRODUCIBLE BY DESIGN

A transparent workflow

The browser application and the accompanying R script follow the same logic so results can be reviewed and reproduced.

  1. 1
    Ingest

    Read CSV records without sending them to a server.

  2. 2
    Standardize

    Map common Spanish and English field names to one schema.

  3. 3
    Derive

    Normalize binary outcomes and assign hydrological season from date.

  4. 4
    Review

    Check missingness, compare outcomes and export analysis-ready data.

Scope and ethics

This is an educational and research data tool, not a clinical diagnostic system. Its summaries do not establish causality, and decisions should follow validated protocols and expert review.
